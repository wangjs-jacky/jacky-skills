FROM FIELD TO DRAWER

Six weeks of patience,
in three steps.

01

Collected at the edges

Our collectors walk field margins, verges and old hedgerows. Never more than one stem in twenty, never from a protected site, always written down where it stood.

31 collectors · 9 countries · GPS + altitude
02

Pressed for six weeks

Between blotting paper and cedar boards, the blotters changed every third day. Slow drying keeps the colour — rushed plants turn brown and brittle.

42 days · blotters changed ×14
03

Mounted, labelled, filed

Stitched to cotton rag with linen thread, labelled in pencil. The specimen and its field note travel together, ready for your drawer.

Cotton rag · linen thread · one field note
